# Prompt para Buzzy IA — Cortometraje "La Mantis de GEMS Hub"

## Contexto rápido (por qué está armado así)
- La mantis es el logo/mascota de GEMS Hub: geométrica low-poly, nunca realista.
- Paleta ÚNICA: violeta `#7c3aed`, lila claro `#a78bfa`, blanco, sobre fondo navy casi negro `#0d0e1a`. Nada de dorado, verde, rojo (salvo alertas puntuales de la historia).
- Historia inventada: un equipo de trabajo se ahoga en caos (WhatsApp, tareas perdidas, todo desconectado) — la mantis aparece como guardián/sistema operativo y ordena el caos en tiempo real.

---

## PROMPT (cópialo tal cual a Buzzy IA)

**Title:** GEMS HUB — The Mantis Awakens (short film, ~60–90s)

**Visual style:** Cinematic 3D render, low-poly geometric art style (faceted triangular surfaces, sharp crystalline edges — like a gemstone sculpture, not a realistic creature). Dramatic volumetric lighting, particle effects, dark sci-fi/cosmic atmosphere. Think "Tron meets a geode" — elegant, futuristic, premium tech-brand feel. Color palette strictly limited to: deep violet-purple (#7c3aed), light lavender (#a78bfa), white highlights, and a near-black navy background (#0d0e1a). No other hues except small red warning-light accents in the chaos scene.

**The Mantis (main character):** A majestic praying mantis made entirely of low-poly geometric facets, like a living gemstone sculpture. Its body is faceted purple and lavender crystal, with sharp triangular planes that catch light like cut glass. Elongated triangular head with large compound eyes made of hundreds of tiny glowing facets. Two long, thin antennae arcing upward, glowing faintly at the tips. Powerful raptorial forearms (the classic mantis "praying" claws), folded and serrated, rendered as sharp jagged purple crystal blades. Slender segmented thorax and abdomen tapering backward. It moves with slow, deliberate, almost regal grace — never insect-like or skittish, more like an ancient guardian entity. When it moves, thin trails of purple light and floating triangular shards drift off its body like embers.

**Setting:** A vast dark cosmic void representing a company's digital workspace — think deep space crossed with a corrupted server room. Floating debris everywhere: shattered chat bubbles, scattered sticky notes, red pinging notification icons, tangled glowing threads representing disconnected tools, all drifting chaotically in the dark.

---

### STORY (3 acts)

**ACT 1 — The Chaos (0:00–0:20)**
Camera drifts through the dark void. Everything is fragmented and disconnected: dozens of small red-glowing message bubbles ping frantically and scatter in every direction; loose glowing threads snap and dangle uselessly; a handful of small dim human silhouettes made of faint white wireframe light drift helplessly, reaching for the chaos, unable to grab hold of anything. The mood is anxious, entropic, overwhelming — a team drowning in disorder. Sound: dissonant pings, overlapping notification chimes, low ominous hum.

**ACT 2 — The Mantis Arrives (0:20–0:50)**
A single point of violet light ignites in the deepest part of the void. Low-poly triangular shards rush inward from every direction and assemble themselves — plate by plate, facet by facet — into the towering silhouette of the Mantis. It opens its faceted compound eyes; they flare with a wave of violet light that sweeps outward like a scanning pulse, and every scattered fragment in the void briefly lights up, as if being read and understood (this is "La Visión" — instant clarity). The Mantis raises its raptorial arms and begins to move through the chaos with slow, powerful, deliberate strikes — each strike doesn't destroy, it *organizes*: a swipe sends a cluster of chat-bubble fragments snapping into a neat glowing grid (a kanban board made of light); another gesture pulls the dangling broken threads together into a single clean glowing line connecting the human silhouettes to each other. The antennae glow brighter with each decision, as if thinking ahead (this is "La Mente" — the AI). The red warning pings fade out one by one as order spreads.

**ACT 3 — Order Restored (0:50–1:20)**
The camera pulls back to reveal the full void transformed: what was chaotic wreckage is now a vast, elegant, structured constellation of glowing violet panels, boards, and connected nodes — clean, geometric, calm — orbiting peacefully around the Mantis, who now stands centered and still, wings settling like a cape, in a posture of quiet guardianship. The small white human silhouettes now stand together in a small circle of light, calm, connected, no longer scattered. The Mantis lowers its head slightly toward them — a gesture of protection, not menace. Final beat: the Mantis's silhouette and the glowing structured grid around it slowly resolve into the GEMS HUB wordmark, rendered in clean white sans-serif type, with a small violet glow pulse. Fade to the navy background with the logo centered.

**Camera direction:** Slow, sweeping cinematic camera moves (dolly-ins, slow orbits). Heavy use of depth of field and volumetric light shafts. Epic, restrained pacing — no fast cuts. Final shot is a locked-off symmetrical composition on the logo reveal.

**Audio mood:** Begins tense and dissonant (anxious synth pads, scattered notification chimes), transitions through a rising orchestral-electronic swell as the Mantis acts, resolves into a calm, confident, minimal ambient tone under the logo reveal — modern, premium, "tech guardian" feel, not aggressive or militaristic.

**Negative prompt / avoid:** realistic insect textures, biological gore, horror tone, any color outside the violet/lavender/white/navy palette (except the fading red pings in Act 1), fast frantic editing, cute/cartoonish rendering, text other than the final logo.
